## What the video shows

One synthetic wallet-paid BAXI trip, from both roles: map-based origin and destination, public-place search, service price and fare details, payment choice, request, driver availability, offer and acceptance, assigned vehicle, start, completion and settlement, receipts, two-way ratings, history and wallet balances.

The recorded fare is **42,000 toman**, commission **8,400**, and driver net **33,600**. The passenger's demo wallet goes from **300,000** to **258,000 toman**. Both ratings are five stars. This recording is a different trip from the 43,000-toman journey in the driver screenshot gallery.

## Capture and typography

The footage consists of genuine browser captures with native timestamps, sampled at approximately **8–10 frames per second** and encoded into a **30 fps** edit. Interface transitions, map movement, search and saved trip states come from actual app interactions. Login, idle operator time and role switching are cut from the edit.

Editorial headings, role badges, captions, title cards and cover use **Pinar DS4 Regular and Bold**, rendered with native browser Persian shaping. The captured interface retains the app's Vazirmatn typography. No font binaries are redistributed in this media folder. The background music is an original synthesized ambient composition.

The source captures and editing scratch files remain local. The published MP4 files were fully decoded without errors; frame size, duration and output frame rate were checked. The cover and all 22 edited compositions were visually reviewed, with no editorial text extending outside the frame.

## Scope

This is an educational product demonstration with synthetic accounts, illustrative tariffs and simulated payments. It shows one successful passenger journey; it does not claim coverage of cancellation, other services, error paths, HR work, physical devices or production readiness. Driver and HR states are documented separately in the [screenshot galleries](../screenshots/README.md). Maps show real OpenStreetMap tiles; the connecting line represents straight-line distance, not a street route. See [map attribution](../map.md) and [pricing policy](../pricing.fa.md).
